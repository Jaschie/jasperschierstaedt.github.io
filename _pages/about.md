# Welcome

I am an MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow in the Environmental Microbiome Engineering group at KU Leuven, working in the laboratory of Prof. Dirk Springael.

My research focuses on the ecology and evolution of microbial communities, with a particular interest in mobile genetic elements, insertion sequences, microbial adaptation, and plant-associated microbiomes. I combine experimental microbiology, microbial ecology, genomics, and bioinformatics to understand how microorganisms evolve and interact in complex environments.

## Research Interests

- Mobile genetic elements and insertion sequences
- Microbial evolution and adaptation
- Environmental microbiology
- Plant-microbiome interactions
- Rhizosphere ecology
- Metagenomics and comparative genomics
- Long-read sequencing and bioinformatics

## Current Position

**MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow**  
Environmental Microbiome Engineering Laboratory  
Department of Microbial and Molecular Systems (M2S)  
KU Leuven, Belgium

## Research Vision

Microbial communities play critical roles in agriculture, ecosystem functioning, and environmental sustainability. My research seeks to understand how evolutionary processes and mobile genetic elements shape microbial adaptation in complex environments, ultimately contributing to the development of more sustainable agricultural systems.
